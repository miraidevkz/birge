# ADR-0016: In-app chat

Status: Accepted · Date: 2026-10-27

## Context
District group chats were planned in a third-party messenger, which stores data outside the jurisdiction and is not allowed. Members also need private conversations after a tea match or an SOS response without exchanging phone numbers. The audience is vulnerable; chats must support crisis detection and moderation.

## Decision
- Own `chat` bounded context in the Go backend. No Matrix or other federated protocol.
- Rooms: district (verified members of the district), topic (by special-needs type), direct after a mutual tea match, direct after the mother opens contact to an SOS responder. Members join group rooms themselves; nothing is joined automatically, because a topic room reveals the child's diagnosis.
- No end-to-end encryption: TLS in transit, room-key encryption at rest. The server can read messages so that the crisis lexicon, filters, reports and moderation work. This is stated in the consent text.
- Delivery: WebSocket while the app is open, neutral push otherwise. Client-generated UUIDv7 message IDs for idempotency; server-assigned per-room sequence for ordering; resume from the last sequence; at-least-once with deduplication. PostgreSQL (time-partitioned) is the source of truth, NATS fans out per room; the gateway is stateless.
- Safety: the crisis lexicon checks every message; a hit sends a private crisis response to the author and an escalation to moderators, never a public signal. Filters hold messages with links or phone numbers (scams, fundraising). Posting is post-moderated with holds: new members' first messages, filter hits. Rate limits and slow mode; report, block user, mute and ban.
- Moderators: team moderators have full rights; mentors moderate their own room with limited rights (hide a message, mute for an hour) and no access to personal data. All actions are audited.
- Privacy: display name only, never phone; no read receipts or typing indicators by default; per-room mute; quiet hours.
- Text only in the first version; photos after the media pipeline (ADR-0015); voice in stage 2.
- Retention (country config): group rooms 1 year, direct rooms 90 days; messages under an open report or escalation are kept until closed. A deleted account leaves "message deleted" (per-user key destroyed).

## Consequences
The chat replaces phone-number exchange for tea and SOS: contacts are never revealed by the platform. Load testing of the WebSocket gateway is part of acceptance.

# ADR-0023: Crisis path and escalations

Status: Accepted · Date: 2026-10-27

## Context
A mother in crisis must reach a person quickly, whatever fails around her: the AI provider, push delivery, a staff member who does not answer. Requirements: § 5 (pipeline, fixed crisis response), § 6 (escalation queue), 15-minute backup.

## Decision
- **Context `safety`** owns crisis detection (level-1 lexicon), escalations and the duty roster. Other contexts (assistant, wellbeing, emergency, chat) call its exported service; none implements its own crisis logic.
- **Lexicon** (`safety/crisis`): normalisation (case, Kazakh and Russian letter variants, repeated letters, common typos) then phrase and pattern matching per language. The production lexicon is data loaded from a path in configuration and kept outside the public repository (gaps: open lexicon); the repository has a small demo lexicon and the evaluation sets (ADR-0017).
- **Escalation lifecycle:** `open → taken → closed`. Opening is idempotent per member while one is open. The open escalation, its `safety.EscalationOpened` event, the alert and a 15-minute timer are created in one transaction. Taking cancels the timer; the timer firing publishes `safety.EscalationUnattended` and alerts the backup shift.
- **Alerts to staff** go through `notify` as critical deliveries to `staff_role` recipients. Safety resolves a role to the current shifts of the duty roster; each shift is an external recipient (its on-duty phone, stored encrypted), so the alert is an SMS sent immediately, independent of the admin app being open. The admin queue is the second channel.
- **No personal data in the queue.** The list shows source, time and district only. Name and phone are revealed only to the staff member who took the escalation, by an explicit call that writes an audit entry (`revealEscalationContact`, `Cache-Control: no-store`).
- **External plan links (addendum 2026-10-28).** An external trusted contact has no account, so the plan SMS carries `<public URL>/plan#<token>`; the token sits in the URL fragment and never reaches a server log. It is 32 random bytes, minted by the worker when notify sends that SMS (so it is never in an event, the outbox or notify's tables) and stored only as HMAC-SHA256 under `BIRGE_PLAN_LINK_KEY_FILE`. A link works for 24 hours from the plan send and is revoked by a new send, by removing the contact and by a newer link for her; unknown, expired and revoked tokens get the same 404 after one keyed-hash lookup. The anonymous `viewExternalPlan` is rate limited per client IP, every view is audited with the contact and owner ids (never the token or IP), and an expired link row is deleted 7 days after expiry by a platform timer (its retention period); account deletion removes her links at once.
- **SOS for trusted contacts and moderators (addendum 2026-10-28).** A confirmed member trusted contact reads her owner's SOS of any type, childcare included, and answers "I can help" with the same response row the district feed uses, so the owner chooses whom to open a private chat with exactly as for neighbours (N5: nobody outside the confirmed contacts can respond to a childcare SOS). Every read is audited before the text is opened. An invited member may decline (or later leave), which is the owner's removal without a notification or a reason. The SOS queue (requirements section 6, "Seen" and "Close") is for moderators and admins with MFA, deny by default; every SOS shown, seen or closed by staff is audited with the staff ID. The first "Seen" is kept; a staff close has the author's effects (timer cancelled, out of the feed, `emergency.SosClosed`) and records the staff ID in `closed_by`. A staff member's repeated view of the same SOS within 10 minutes (the admin polls the queue every 30 seconds) is not audited again; the first view and every Seen and Close are.
- **External SOS links (addendum 2026-10-29).** Requirement § 4.6: an external trusted contact gets the SOS "by SMS, no content, with a link". The SOS SMS carries `<public URL>/sos-link#<token>` (not `/sos`, the mother's own SOS screen), minted like a plan link at send time and stored only as HMAC-SHA256 under the same key, over `"emergency.sos_link|" + token`, so a plan token never opens an SOS and the reverse. SOS links have their own table (`emergency.sos_links`), not a kind column on `plan_links`: they live as long as the SOS (closed, auto-closed, at most 24 hours from the raise) rather than the plan send, and every plan query would otherwise need a kind filter whose omission lets one kind open the other. The view re-checks the contact (active, external, confirmed, still hers) and the SOS (open, before auto-close) in its single lookup; all failures are one 404; every view is audited (`emergency_sos.view_external`, actor = contact id); retention and account deletion are as for plan links. **The SOS SMS is critical and never waits for its link:** if the link cannot be made (not found, an error, a slow mint), notify sends the template's text without the link (`<template>.no_link`) at once instead of skipping or retrying. The page shows her first name, the type, the text and the times, with the "call 112" banner, and asks the contact to get in touch the way she usually does (she has no account, so there is no "I can help").
- **The client never waits for the server** to show the crisis screen and phone numbers; `requestHumanHelp` only adds a person.

## Consequences
The duty roster must always have a primary and a backup shift; a monitoring alert fires when the next 24 hours are not covered. Staff authentication (OIDC) is required before the staff operations can be used.
